Every webhook now records the teammate who created it. A new **Created by** column in **Settings** > **Webhooks** shows that person at a glance, and it appears on each webhook's detail page too.

This is another step toward bringing webhooks to parity with [Automations](/features/automations), which already show their creator.

## What changed

- The Webhooks list has a **Created by** column.
- Each webhook's detail page shows its creator alongside the rest of its configuration.
- Webhooks created before this change show **Unknown**, the same way older automations do.
