Adapt's integration library has grown significantly. We've added eleven new connectors spanning data warehouses, revenue tools, engineering systems, and customer platforms — bringing the total to 20+ supported integrations.

## New integrations

### Data & Analytics

**Snowflake** — Query your data warehouse directly. Ask questions against tables and views without writing SQL. Adapt understands your schema and translates natural language into accurate queries.

**Databricks** — Connect to your Databricks workspace to query Delta tables, explore datasets, and pull analytics from your lakehouse.

### Payments & Revenue

**Stripe** — Pull revenue metrics, subscription data, invoice history, and customer payment details. Ask questions like "What's our MRR trend over the past 6 months?" or "Which customers have failed payments this week?"

### CRM & Sales

**Attio** — Query your Attio CRM for contact, company, and deal data. Combine with other integrations to enrich records or surface insights across your full pipeline.

**Clay** — Connect Clay to pull enriched prospect and contact data into your Adapt queries.

### Engineering & Product

**Sentry** — Search errors, track issue volume, and correlate incidents with releases or deployments. Ask "What errors spiked after yesterday's deploy?" and get a direct answer.

**Notion** — Search and read Notion pages. Useful for pulling internal documentation, strategy docs, or team wikis into broader research queries.

### Support & Messaging

**Intercom** — Query conversations, user data, and support ticket history. Understand common issues, track resolution rates, or pull context on specific customers.

**Zendesk** — Connect your Zendesk instance to analyze ticket volume, response times, and recurring themes across your support queue.

### Marketing & SEO

**Ahrefs** — Pull keyword rankings, backlink data, and organic traffic metrics. Ask questions about your SEO performance without logging into another dashboard.

### Compliance & Security

**Drata** — Access your compliance posture, control status, and audit evidence directly from Adapt.

## How integrations work

Each integration connects through OAuth or an API key — no code required. Once connected, Adapt can query it alongside all your other integrations, joining data across systems in a single response.

See the [Integrations overview](/integrations/overview) for setup instructions for each connector.
