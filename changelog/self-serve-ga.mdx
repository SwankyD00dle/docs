After months in private beta, Adapt is now generally available. Anyone can sign up at [app.adapt.com](https://app.adapt.com) and be running queries against their own data in minutes.

## What's included in 1.0

This release represents the full Adapt platform we've been building toward. Everything that was available in the beta is now stable, documented, and production-ready.

**Core capabilities:**

- **Ask** — Query your connected data sources in plain language and get structured, cited answers
- **Understand** — Explore trends, anomalies, and relationships across your organization's data
- **Act** — Take action directly from insights: send messages, create records, trigger workflows
- **Scheduled Tasks** — Automate recurring queries and deliver results to Slack or your inbox
- **Apps** — Build and deploy custom dashboards and tools from a conversation
- **Skills** — Extend Adapt with domain-specific knowledge and your organization's workflows

## Self-serve sign-up

Previously, getting access meant a discovery call, a trial setup, and a wait. That's gone. You can now:

1. Sign up with your work email at [app.adapt.com](https://app.adapt.com)
2. Connect your first integration
3. Start asking questions

The free tier includes enough to evaluate Adapt thoroughly before committing to a plan.

## Pricing

Adapt uses usage-based pricing so you pay for what you use, not a seat count that scales with your headcount. See the [pricing overview](/pricing/overview) for details.

## What's next

GA is a milestone, not a stopping point. The roadmap ahead includes deeper integration coverage, team collaboration features, and expanded automation capabilities. Follow the changelog to stay current.
