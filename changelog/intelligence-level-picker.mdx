Adapt still sizes its own intelligence to the work by default. Now you can override it. A picker in the composer lets you pin a chat to **Deep**, **Balanced**, **Fast**, or **Super Fast** when you know what the work needs, and Adapt holds that level for the whole conversation.

## Pick a level

The picker sits in the composer toolbar, next to the attachment and integration buttons. It reads **Auto** until you change it.

![The Adapt composer with the intelligence picker open, showing Auto, Deep, Balanced, Fast, and Super Fast](https://adapt.com/docs/images/changelog/intelligence-level-picker.png)

- **Auto** is the default. Adapt reads each task and matches its intelligence to the work, shifting levels as the work changes character.
- **Deep** puts Adapt's most capable reasoning on hard, open-ended problems.
- **Balanced** handles multi-step reasoning.
- **Fast** and **Super Fast** keep routine, well-defined work snappy.

## It sticks

Pick a level on a new chat and it applies from the first message. Once a level is pinned, Adapt won't re-route the chat on its own, so a conversation you set to Deep stays Deep for every turn.

You can change it mid-conversation from the same picker in an open chat. Switch back to **Auto** to unpin the chat and hand routing back to Adapt.

Which models sit behind each level depends on your organization's [model recipe](/changelog/model-recipes), chosen by owners and admins under **Settings** > **Intelligence**.
