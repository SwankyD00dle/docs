LinkedIn is now two integrations, each connected over OAuth with no API keys to manage. Connect **LinkedIn** for organization pages, social posting, events, and networking, and **LinkedIn Ads** for advertising analytics and campaign management. Splitting them lets you grant exactly the access you need for each use case.

## What's new

- **LinkedIn** — read your organization's pages and engagement, publish posts, and manage events. Pick from a set of optional permissions at connect time so Adapt only gets the access you grant.
- **LinkedIn Ads** — analyze campaign performance, track spend and conversions, and create or update campaigns across your ad accounts.

Both connections refresh their tokens automatically and are shared across your organization.

Learn more in the [LinkedIn](/integrations/linkedin) and [LinkedIn Ads](/integrations/linkedin-ads) docs.
