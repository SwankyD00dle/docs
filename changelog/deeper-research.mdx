Adapt can now go beyond ordinary web search by drawing on specialized, structured data when a question calls for it. It combines those findings with current open-web sources, checks the evidence across both, and returns one answer with citations.

This deeper research is available on paid plans and active pilots. There are no separate accounts or integrations to set up, and premium sources are billed in credits as part of your normal Adapt usage.

![Adapt researching a person across specialized data and open-web sources](https://adapt.com/docs/images/changelog/deeper-web-research.png)

## What you can research

- **Companies and people:** Build prospect lists, enrich company profiles, and research professional backgrounds.
- **Markets and competitors:** Compare website traffic, engagement, rankings, and similar companies.
- **Public companies:** Find market data and recent news tied to specific companies and tickers.
- **Business verification:** Check US business registrations, officers, and compliance signals.
- **Products and pricing:** Search product catalogs across brands and merchants.
- **Podcasts:** Find relevant episodes and pull transcript excerpts with speakers and timestamps.
- **Travel:** Compare destinations and discover airfare from a specific departure point.

## How it works

Ask a research question as usual. Adapt selects the sources that fit the task, searches the open web in parallel for verification and context, and combines the results into a cited response. For more complex work, it can use several data types in the same task and return the findings as a report, table, or other structured output.

Try prompts like:

```
Compare our top five competitors by website traffic, engagement, and recent market activity.
```

```
Build a list of US companies that match our ICP and verify each business before adding it.
```

```
Find recent podcast discussions about AI adoption in finance and pull the most relevant quotes.
```

Learn more about [web and deep research](https://adapt.com/docs/features/understand#web-research).
