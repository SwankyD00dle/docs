GPT-6 Astra is now supported in the OpenAI frontier recipe for Deep intelligence. The update does not switch your organization or personal recipe selection.

![Intelligence settings showing the OpenAI stack with Astra frontier selected](https://adapt.com/docs/images/changelog/frontier-intelligence-update.png)

## Review your active recipe

Owners and admins can set the **Organization default**. To choose a different recipe for your own work, turn on **Personal recipe** and select a recipe card. Your personal choice takes precedence over the organization default; turn it off to follow the organization default again.

Compare each recipe's recommendation and relative cost indicator before choosing. Adapt continues to match intelligence to the task when your chat is set to **Auto**.

Learn more about [intelligence recipes](/getting-started/core-concepts#model-recipes).
