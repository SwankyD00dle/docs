Every conversation you have with Adapt is now automatically saved. Your full history is accessible from the sidebar, searchable by content, and private to you unless you choose to share.

## Private by default

Chats belong to the person who started them. No one else in your organization can see your conversations unless you explicitly share them. This matters in practice — when you're exploring sensitive data, working through early-stage ideas, or troubleshooting a problem, you shouldn't have to think about who else might be watching.

Admins can see metadata (when chats occurred, with which integrations) for compliance purposes, but not content.

## What's saved

Every chat stores:

- The full conversation thread, including all messages and Adapt's responses
- Sources and citations Adapt referenced to generate answers
- Any apps, tasks, or actions created during the conversation
- A timestamp and the integrations that were active

## Finding past chats

The sidebar now shows your recent conversations. Use the search bar to find any chat by keyword — Adapt searches across message content, so you can find a conversation by what was discussed, not just what you named it.

You can also filter by date range or by which integration was involved.

## Sharing a chat

When you want to share context with a colleague, open the chat and click **Share**. You'll get a link that grants read-only access to that specific conversation. Recipients don't need to be in your organization to view a shared chat.

## Continuing where you left off

Reopening a saved chat resumes it fully. Adapt has the complete context of everything discussed, so you don't need to re-explain the situation or repeat prior instructions. Ask a follow-up and it picks up seamlessly.

## Deleting chats

Chats can be deleted from the conversation menu. Deletion is permanent and removes all stored content, including any citations and generated artifacts tied to that conversation.
