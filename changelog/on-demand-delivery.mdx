Automations could already deliver to your email or your Messages thread. Now any chat can.

Ask for it in the moment and Adapt sends it:

```
Email me this cost breakdown so I can forward it to finance.
```

```
Text me the three blockers from this thread before I get on the plane.
```

The answer still appears where you asked. The email or text is a copy you can act on away from your desk — with a link back to the full chat.

## Route the whole chat

Set a destination once and every response in that chat follows it:

```
Send my responses in this chat to my email from now on.
```

Three things worth knowing:

- The preference is per person. In a shared chat, teammates keep their own destinations.
- The current answer stays where it started. Ask Adapt to email or text it too if you want it immediately.
- **Automations you create in that chat inherit the destination.** Route a chat to email, then say _"every weekday at 7:30am, summarize overnight Sentry errors and open Linear bugs for the API service"_ — the daily summary lands in your inbox instead of the channel you happened to be standing in.

Say **origin** to go back to answering wherever each message came from.

## Getting set up

Email needs a verified account email. Messages needs a connected phone and an existing text thread with Adapt. Adapt checks before it sends and tells you when a destination isn't ready.

One limitation: replying to a text Adapt sent you starts a new conversation rather than continuing the chat it came from. Reply threading is next.
