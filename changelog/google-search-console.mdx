You can now connect Google Search Console to Adapt with one-click OAuth — no API keys to copy or rotate. Once connected, Adapt can read your search queries, clicks, impressions, and ranking position so you can report on search performance alongside the rest of your data.

## How it works

**Connect in one click.** Go to **Settings → Integrations → Google Search Console** and click **Add**. Sign in with Google and approve read-only access. Adapt manages the credentials and refreshes the token automatically.

**No property picker.** The connection can read every Search Console property the account has access to, and Adapt picks the right one based on your question. You can connect multiple Google accounts if your properties are split across them.

**Ask away.** Once connected, ask things like "How many clicks did we get from Google Search last week?" or "What are our top search queries this month?"

See the [Google Search Console integration docs](https://adapt.com/docs/integrations/google-search-console) for setup details.
