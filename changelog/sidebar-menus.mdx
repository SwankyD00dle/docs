The sidebar now organizes everything behind two clean menus: a **workspace menu** at the top for your organization, and a **user menu** at the bottom for your personal settings and support. Instead of hunting through a single crowded list, each menu groups the things that belong together, so getting to the right place takes fewer clicks.

## Workspace menu

Click your organization name at the top of the sidebar to switch organizations and jump straight to any org-level page. The current organization is marked with a checkmark, and every organization page is one click away:

- **Settings** — general organization settings
- **Members**, **Groups**, **Integrations**, **Policies**
- **Knowledge**, **Skills**, **Usage**

![Workspace menu open in the Adapt sidebar, showing an Organizations list with a checkmark on the current org, and links to Settings, Members, Groups, Integrations, Policies, Knowledge, Skills, and Usage](https://adapt.com/docs/images/changelog/sidebar-menus-workspace.png)

## User menu

Click your name and avatar at the bottom of the sidebar for everything personal:

- **Account**, **Preferences**, **Authentication**, and **Invites** — with a dot on **Invites** when you have a pending invitation
- **Support** — a submenu with live system status, a **New support case** form, links to **Documentation** and **Email support**, a way to reach sales, and — when Slack is connected — a Slack Connect request
- **Log out**

![User menu open in the Adapt sidebar, showing Account, Preferences, Authentication, and Invites, plus an expanded Support submenu with system status, New support case, Documentation, Email support, pricing questions, and Connect on Slack](https://adapt.com/docs/images/changelog/sidebar-menus-user.png)

Support now lives in this menu as a submenu, so help is always one hover away from wherever you're working.
