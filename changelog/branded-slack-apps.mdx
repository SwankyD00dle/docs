## How it works

- Customize your app's name and description in **Settings > Integrations > Slack**.
- Create the app in Slack with a ready-made manifest, then paste your credentials back into Adapt.
- Authorize the app and invite it to your channels. Your team tags your bot by its new name — @yourcompany — and gets the full Adapt experience: answers, actions, automations, and proactive responses.

## Notes

- The branded app and the Adapt app can be connected to the same Slack workspace at the same time. Conversations that start with your branded bot stay with it.
- Your branded app is a Slack app you own, created from Adapt's manifest, powered by the same Adapt agent. Capabilities are unchanged.
- To update branding later, rename the app inside Slack under App Home and Display Information; the name shown in Slack follows the Slack app, not the settings saved in Adapt.
