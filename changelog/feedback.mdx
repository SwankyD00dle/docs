There's now a **Feedback** button in the sidebar. Click it to open a modal, type a message about anything — bugs you've hit, features you want, rough edges in the experience — and hit send.

## What it's for

This is a direct line to the Adapt team about your experience with the product. Use it for:

- Bugs and unexpected behavior
- Feature requests
- UX friction or confusing flows
- General observations about how Adapt is (or isn't) working for you

## How submissions are handled

Every submission is stored and posted to the Adapt team's internal `#feedback` Slack channel in real time. A background job runs each submission through an LLM to flag actionable items and surface recommendations to the team, so nothing gets lost.

We read every message.

## How to send feedback

1. Click the **Feedback** button in the sidebar
2. Write your message in the modal
3. Press **Send feedback** (or `Cmd/Ctrl + Enter`)

That's it. No forms, no tickets, no triage queue you have to navigate.
