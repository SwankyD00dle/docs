A bundle of changes to make Adapt cheaper to start with, cheaper to run, and longer-lasting once credits are in your account. 1 credit is still $0.01 before bulk discounts, but each credit now goes further: more questions answered, more scheduled tasks run, more apps built for the same balance.

## What changed

**Your credits now go further.** Adapt has gotten meaningfully more capable over the last few months, and we're passing that gain on. Every $0.01 credit now does more work — more questions answered, more scheduled tasks run, more apps built — on the same balance you already have. No plan change, no action needed.

**Free-email signups now get $25 in credits**, up from $10. Business-email signups stay at $100 in signup credits.

**Free-email signups also earn the Slack bonus.** Connecting Slack grants $100 in credits to every new organization, regardless of email domain. The other integration bonuses ($25 each for GitHub, Google Workspace, Linear, Notion, HubSpot) and the $50 invite-teammates bonus remain business-email only.

**Promo credits last 6 months.** Going forward, signup credits, integration bonuses, and invite-teammate bonuses now expire 180 days after they're granted, up from 30 days. 

**New $50/mo entry plan.** A new self-serve tier starts at $50/mo for 5,000 credits, or $540/year for 60,000 credits (10% annual discount). It sits below the existing $100/mo plan and is available now in the upgrade modal under **Settings → Billing**.
