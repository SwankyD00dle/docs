Adapt now decides how much intelligence each task needs. Simple, well-defined work runs **Fast**. Hard, open-ended problems run **Deep**, with Adapt's most capable reasoning. You don't pick a mode or a model. Adapt reads the work in front of it and matches its intelligence to the task.

## How it works

Every request makes different demands. A quick lookup, a long synthesis, a tricky debugging problem, and a costly-to-get-wrong decision each call for a different level of intelligence. Adaptive Intelligence sizes that automatically and shifts as the work changes character, even within a single task.

A quick lookup runs Fast:

![Adapt running Fast for a simple request to find the last Netlify deploy time](https://adapt.com/docs/images/changelog/adaptive-intelligence-fast.png)

A hard, open-ended reasoning problem goes Deep:

![Adapt running Deep while reasoning through a tricky distributed-systems design question](https://adapt.com/docs/images/changelog/adaptive-intelligence-deep.png)

This happens behind the scenes on every request, including the work sub-agents do in parallel. There's nothing to configure.

## Why it matters

- **Better answers on the hard stuff.** When a problem is genuinely difficult, Adapt reaches for its deepest reasoning instead of rushing it.
- **Fast where speed wins.** Routine work stays quick and snappy. You're not waiting on heavy reasoning for a simple lookup.
- **Always state-of-the-art.** Under the hood, Adapt draws on best-in-class frontier models from Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI, and folds in new ones as they arrive. You get the best of the frontier without ever picking a model.
- **No knobs to turn.** You ask the same way you always have. Adapt handles the rest.

## Availability

Adaptive Intelligence is live for every workspace. No setup required. It applies to all chats, scheduled tasks, apps, and sub-agents automatically.
